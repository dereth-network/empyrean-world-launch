# Changes

## 0.6.0 (2026-11-26)

- Follows ac1999 to `b25a9e0a`: the landscape's spawns are redrawn by biome and moved onto spots the 1999
  land can use, towns away from the starter towns hold only harmless creatures, a score of casters get
  their spellbooks, and the Candeth Keep placements go.

## 0.5.0 (2026-11-23)

- Follows ac1999 to `183e74f8`: town portals carry their old names and colors, landscape spawns move
  (Shadow Wisps back, Drudge Robbers off, Virindi and Obsidian Golems to the Obsidian Plains), creatures
  drop more loot, and Fort Tethana's, Wai Jhou's and Arwic's people and animals are put right.
- And from `ee3a6833`, which was never released on its own: looted armor takes more varied colors,
  dinnerware is valuables loot in materials instead of a thrown weapon, and creature treasure is retuned.

## 0.3.0 (2026-11-08)

- Follows ac1999 to `3c564fdf`: doors carry their heritage names again, such as "Aluvian-Door".

## 0.2.0 (2026-11-06)

- Follows ac1999 to `e4eb0378`: Lich's Place is restored, and Shadows leave the general landscape.

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-04)

- First world base: ac1999 at `80adf514`, for the Launch era on the 1999 data files.
